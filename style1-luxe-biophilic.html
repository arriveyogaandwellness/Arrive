Meet Your Facilitator

I’m Carly Anne — a yoga educator, entrepreneur, and someone who believes in the power of showing up honestly as yourself. For over ten years, I’ve been working in the wellness space, helping people reconnect with their bodies, breath, and deeper sense of self.
