# Arrive Yoga & Wellness Ecosystem — Corporate Charter & Executive Overview

## 1. Company Identity & Vision
- **Legal Entities:** Arrive Yoga & Wellness Center LLC | The Art of Arrival Retreats | Arrive Consulting | Yogis Give Back (Philanthropic Initiative)
- **Founder & CEO:** Carly Anne Kasinpila
- **Headquarters:** United States (Operating Internationally)
- **Core Methodology:** The ARRIVE Method™ (*Anchor, Reveal, Rewrite, Integrate, Voice, Embody*)
- **Core Philosophy:** *"Every Body Is A Yoga Body"*

### Mission Statement
To create transformational spaces where people heal, awaken, and lead with integrity through somatic movement, trauma-aware nervous system regulation, luxury wellness retreats, and purpose-driven community impact.

### Vision Statement
To establish Arrive Yoga as the premiere global standard for somatic wellness retreats, somatic teacher training, and conscious executive leadership.
