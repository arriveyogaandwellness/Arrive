The ARRIVE Method™ — 6 Somatic Pillars
A — Anchor (Earth)

Grounding the body, stabilizing diaphragmatic breath, cultivating baseline nervous system safety.

R — Reveal (Water)

Noticing sensations, emotional holdings, and truth without judgment or performative critique.

R — Rewrite (Fire)

Transforming old narratives into self-respecting boundaries and empowered neuroplastic choices.

I — Integrate (Heart)

Translating peak shifts off the mat into enduring daily behavior and somatic co-regulation.

V — Voice (Air)

Vagal toning, vocal affirmations, authentic communication, and throat chakra resonance.

E — Embody (Avatar)

Living the practice through fluid movement, posture, presence, relationships, and leadership.
