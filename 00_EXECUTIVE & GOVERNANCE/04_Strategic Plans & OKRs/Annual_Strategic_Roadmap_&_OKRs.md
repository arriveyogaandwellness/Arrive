# Arrive Yoga — 3-Year Strategic Roadmap & OKRs

## Year 1 Objectives (Foundation & Flagship Retreats)
- **Objective 1 (Retreat Excellence):** Successfully sell out 2 international cohorts of *The Art of Arrival* at Amanti Resort, Costa Rica (20–25 guests per cohort).
  - *KR 1:* Generate $125,000+ in gross retreat revenue with a net profit margin exceeding 55%.
  - *KR 2:* Maintain a 95%+ guest satisfaction score (NPS 75+).
  - *KR 3:* Collect 15+ video testimonials and professional 4K media library.
- **Objective 2 (Brand & Methodology):** Trademark *The ARRIVE Method™* and standardize curriculum.
  - *KR 1:* File USPTO trademark applications for core brand assets.
  - *KR 2:* Publish the 100-page ARRIVE Method Facilitator Manual.
- **Objective 3 (Philanthropy):** Fully operationalize *Yogis Give Back*.
  - *KR 1:* Fund local Costa Rican rainforest reforestation / marine conservation partner with 1% of gross revenue.

## Year 2 Objectives (Scale & Teacher Training)
- Launch the 200-Hour ARRIVE Somatic Yoga Teacher Training (Hybrid Online + Costa Rica Immersion).
- Expand retreat cadence to 4 cohorts annually (Costa Rica, Portugal, Bali).
- Launch the ARRIVE Digital Membership portal for post-retreat somatic integration.

## Year 3 Objectives (Corporate Wellness & Global Recognition)
- Establish B2B Corporate Wellness Division for executive leadership retreats.
- Partner with luxury eco-resorts across 3 continents.
- Achieve $1,000,000 in annual ecosystem gross revenue.
