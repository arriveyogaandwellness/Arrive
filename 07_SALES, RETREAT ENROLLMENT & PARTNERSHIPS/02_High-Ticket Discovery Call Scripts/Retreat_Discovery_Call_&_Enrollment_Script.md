# High-Ticket Somatic Discovery Call & Enrollment Script

### Stage 1: Somatic Connection & Rapport (First 5 Mins)
- "Welcome [Name]. Before we dive into logistics or suites, let's take one collective breath together. Where are you joining from today?"
- "What inspired you to fill out the application for *The Art of Arrival*?"

### Stage 2: Discovering the Core Need (10 Mins)
- "Tell me about where your nervous system is at right now in your day-to-day life."
- "What have you tried in terms of wellness or yoga, and where has it felt incomplete or performative?"

### Stage 3: Aligning with The ARRIVE Method & Costa Rica (10 Mins)
- Paint the picture: "At Amanti Resort, we have a total buyout—only 20 to 22 guests. You wake up to Scarlet Macaws, do morning somatic breathwork overlooking the canopy, and eat farm-to-table organic meals prepared by our private chef."
- Explain the 6 Pillars: Anchor, Reveal, Rewrite, Integrate, Voice, Embody.

### Stage 4: Suite Selection & Invitation to Enroll (5 Mins)
- "Based on what you've shared, I think the [Queen Suite / Honeymoon Suite] is the best container for you. The total investment is $[Price], which covers everything except your flight."
- "Would you like me to hold your suite right now with your $500 deposit?"
