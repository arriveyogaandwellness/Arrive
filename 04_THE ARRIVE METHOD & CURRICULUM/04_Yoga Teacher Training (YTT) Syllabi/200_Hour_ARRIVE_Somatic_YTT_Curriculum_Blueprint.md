# 200-Hour ARRIVE Somatic Yoga Teacher Training (YTT) — Curriculum Blueprint
**Program Title:** The ARRIVE Method™ 200-Hour Somatic Teacher Certification  
**Lead Educator:** Carly Anne Kasinpila (500 E-RYT, Founder)  
**Format:** Hybrid (100 Hours Live Virtual Immersion + 100 Hours In-Person Costa Rica Practicum at Amanti Resort)

---

## 1. Educational Philosophy: "Every Body Is A Yoga Body"
The ARRIVE YTT moves beyond rote posture memorization. It equips modern teachers with trauma-informed somatic awareness, functional anatomy, vocal resonance, nervous system regulation, and sustainable wellness business acumen.

---

## 2. Core Curriculum Modules (200 Hours Total)

### Module 1: The 6 Somatic Pillars of ARRIVE (50 Hours)
- **Anchor (Earth):** Grounding kinematics, pelvic floor stabilization, nervous system safety cues.
- **Reveal (Water):** Somatic inquiry, body mapping, emotional holding patterns, trauma release.
- **Rewrite (Fire):** Empowered cueing, neuroplasticity, repatterning postural compensation.
- **Integrate (Heart):** Restorative biomechanics, Yoga Nidra, polyvagal theory off the mat.
- **Voice (Air & Throat):** Vagal toning, vocal confidence for teachers, language ethics.
- **Embody (Avatar / Ether):** Fluid sequencing, intuitive alignment, trauma-aware hands-on assists.

### Module 2: Functional Biomechanics & Nervous System Anatomy (40 Hours)
- Fascial lines (Anatomy Trains), joint mobility, and spine health.
- Polyvagal theory: The dorsal vagal, sympathetic, and ventral vagal branches in movement.
- Contraindications, modifications, and inclusive prop architecture for all body types.

### Module 3: The Art of Facilitation & Sonic Curation (30 Hours)
- Vocal modulation, silence, pacing, and breath management.
- 432Hz sound frequency healing, crystal bowl acoustics, and trauma-aware playlist architecture.
- Holding group containers: Managing trauma triggers, collective co-regulation, and boundary ethics.

### Module 4: Costa Rica Practicum & Teaching Labs (50 Hours)
- Daily morning sunrise practicums in the Amanti open-air panoramic shala.
- Peer teaching, live video feedback, personalized 1-on-1 mentorship with Carly Anne.
- Graduation ceremony and sacred Mayan Temazcal initiation.

### Module 5: Wellness Entrepreneurship & Ethics (30 Hours)
- Building an authentic wellness brand: Marketing without burnout or performative gimmicks.
- High-ticket retreat operations, pricing psychology, and legal protection.
- *Yogis Give Back*: Building social impact and philanthropy into your teaching business.
