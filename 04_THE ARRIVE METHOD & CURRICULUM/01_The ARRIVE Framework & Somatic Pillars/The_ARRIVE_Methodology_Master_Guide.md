# The ARRIVE Method™ — Master Pedagogical Framework
*Founded by Carly Anne Kasinpila*

> *"This isn't performative yoga. No flexibility contest. No comparison. It is a guided, trauma-aware human return to yourself."*

---

## The 6 Pillars of ARRIVE

### **A — Anchor**
- **Somatic Function:** Grounding the nervous system, dropping into the sensory body, cultivating baseline safety.
- **Practices:** Diaphragmatic box breathing (4-4-4-4), foot-to-earth sensory rooting, slow spinal waves, safe-space visualization.
- **Guiding Prompt:** *"Where does your body touch the earth right now, and what does it feel like to let yourself be supported?"*

### **R — Reveal**
- **Somatic Function:** Somatic inquiry. Noticing sensations, chronic holding patterns, and emotional truth without critique.
- **Practices:** Yin yoga holds, body scanning, witnessing sensation without immediate narrative attachment.
- **Guiding Prompt:** *"What is present in this sensation if you don't call it good or bad?"*

### **R — Rewrite**
- **Somatic Function:** Neuroplastic somatic repatterning. Shifting stored trauma and old self-limiting beliefs into empowered agency.
- **Practices:** Dynamic somatic shaking, emotional release, boundary-setting postures (Warrior series with intentional gaze).
- **Guiding Prompt:** *"What story are you ready to stop carrying for other people?"*

### **I — Integrate**
- **Somatic Function:** Off-the-mat translation. Turning transient peak experiences into enduring daily somatic behaviors.
- **Practices:** Reflective journaling, restorative yoga nidra, paired authentic relating dialogs.
- **Guiding Prompt:** *"How will this shift show up when you open your front door back home?"*

### **V — Voice**
- **Somatic Function:** Vagal nerve stimulation, throat chakra opening, authentic creative self-expression.
- **Practices:** Tonal humming, pranayama (Brahmari breath), chanting, spoken affirmations, vocal release.
- **Guiding Prompt:** *"What has stayed quiet for too long that is ready to be spoken?"*

### **E — Embody**
- **Somatic Function:** Living the practice in posture, presence, relationships, and leadership.
- **Practices:** Fluid ecstatic movement, celebratory dance, alignment integration.
- **Guiding Prompt:** *"Walk as if you already arrive whole."*
