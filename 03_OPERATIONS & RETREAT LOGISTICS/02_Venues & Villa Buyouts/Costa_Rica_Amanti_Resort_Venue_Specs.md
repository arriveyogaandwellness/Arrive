# Venue Specifications: Amanti Resort & Spa (Playa Hermosa, Costa Rica)

- **Location:** Playa Hermosa, Puntarenas Province, Costa Rica
- **Proximity:** 2 minutes to black sand volcanic beach; 9 minutes to Rainmaker Suspended Bridges; 90 minutes direct scenic highway from SJO Airport.
- **Buyout Capacity:** 22 private air-conditioned bedrooms across private estates and villas.
- **Accommodations Layout:**
  - **Honeymoon Suites:** Private king suite with soaking tub, jungle balcony, private lounge.
  - **Queen Suites:** Individual private bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms.
  - **Macaw Villas (2-Bedroom):** 2 king/queen bedrooms with private dipping pool.
  - **Toucan Villas (3-Bedroom):** 3 spacious suites with wrap-around rainforest deck.
  - **Amapola Estate (5-Bedroom):** Master estate villa with full gourmet kitchen and private pool.
- **Resort Amenities:**
  - Open-air panoramic yoga shala deck framed by Almendra trees (home to wild Scarlet Macaws and Toucans)
  - 2 waterfall swimming pools & 12+ person hydrotherapy jacuzzi
  - Cold immersion plunge tub (ideal for nervous system regulation sessions)
  - Full commercial kitchen with dedicated private chef service
  - 24/7 gated security and private concierge desk
