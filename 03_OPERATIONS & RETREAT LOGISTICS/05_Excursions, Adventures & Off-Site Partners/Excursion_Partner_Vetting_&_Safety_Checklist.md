# Excursion Safety, Insurance & Partner Vetting Checklist
**Department:** 03 Operations & Retreat Logistics | **Host:** Carly Anne Kasinpila

---

## 1. Mandatory Vetting Criteria for Off-Site Partners
Before contracting any third-party excursion partner in Costa Rica (Rainmaker Suspended Bridges, Tortuga Catamaran, Mayan Temazcal, Horseback Riding), the following documents must be verified:

- [ ] **Commercial Liability Insurance:** Proof of active international or local Costa Rican commercial liability policy with minimum $500,000 USD coverage.
- [ ] **ICT (Instituto Costarricense de Turismo) Accreditation:** Verified tourism permit and certified naturalist / adventure guides.
- [ ] **Bilingual Wilderness First Responders (WFR):** At least one bilingual lead guide certified in Wilderness First Aid / CPR on every excursion.
- [ ] **Communication Equipment:** Satellite phone or reliable VHF two-way radio carried on remote excursions where cell reception drops.

---

## 2. Pre-Departure Safety Briefing (Led by Host Carly Anne)
Before any group leaves Amanti Resort for an off-site excursion:
1. **Hydration Check:** Ensure every attendee has at least 1.5L of water in an insulated reusable bottle.
2. **Footwear Inspection:** Verify appropriate closed-toe trail shoes or strap adventure sandals.
3. **Medical Bag Check:** Ensure the Retreat Operations Director carries the mobile trauma kit, guest allergy manifest, and emergency satellite beacon.
4. **Buddy System:** Pair guests in twos; verify all guests check in prior to return shuttle departure.
