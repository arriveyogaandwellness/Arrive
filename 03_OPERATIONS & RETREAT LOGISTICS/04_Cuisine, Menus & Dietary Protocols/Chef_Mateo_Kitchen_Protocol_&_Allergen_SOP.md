## 3. Strict Allergen Management & Cross-Contamination Protocols
1. **Separate Prep Stations:** Dedicated cutting boards, knives, and prep areas for gluten-free and nut-free dishes.
2. **Guest Name Cards on Plates:** All plated meals for guests with severe allergies must feature their name card and allergen tag.
3. **Daily Manifest Cross-Check:** Review the updated `Dietary_Requirements_&_Allergy_Tracker.csv` at 07:00 AM every morning during the morning staff standup.
4. **Kitchen First Aid:** An EpiPen and emergency antihistamines are permanently located in the primary first-aid cabinet next to the pantry.
