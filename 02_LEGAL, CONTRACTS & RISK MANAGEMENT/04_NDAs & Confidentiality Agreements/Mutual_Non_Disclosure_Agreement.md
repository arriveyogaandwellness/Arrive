# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into between **Arrive Yoga & Wellness Center LLC** and **[Counterparty Name / Company]** to protect confidential proprietary business models, financial models, curriculum frameworks, and guest databases.

1. **Confidential Information:** Includes financial statements, unit economics, curriculum guides (*The ARRIVE Method™*), guest personal health data, and strategic expansion plans.
