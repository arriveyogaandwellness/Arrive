# Retreat Assistant Facilitator & Team Onboarding Packet
**Arrive Yoga & Wellness Retreats — The Art of Arrival**

---

## 1. Welcome to the Facilitation Team!
Thank you for joining Carly Anne Kasinpila and the Arrive Yoga leadership team in Costa Rica. As an assistant facilitator, you are the grounding anchor that allows our guests to feel safe, held, and fully supported.

---

## 2. Core Expectations & Professional Conduct
- **Presence & Punctuality:** Arrive at the Shala 25 minutes prior to every session. Ensure mats, props, water carafes, and sound equipment are pristine.
- **Trauma-Aware Language:** Always request verbal or non-verbal consent before offering physical adjustments or assists. Honor every participant's autonomy.
- **Confidentiality:** Personal emotional releases, journal shares, and medical disclosures are sacred and strictly confidential.
- **Zero Substance Policy:** No alcohol, marijuana, or recreational substances are permitted for staff during active retreat operating hours.

---

## 3. Daily Staff Rhythm
- **06:15 AM:** Morning team alignment with Carly Anne (5-minute breath check & Shala staging).
- **01:00 PM:** Lunch debrief (check on guest energy, room needs, or emerging emotional support).
- **09:30 PM:** Evening wrap-up & lights out.
