# Costa Rica Welcome Packet & Luxury Packing Guide
**The Art of Arrival Retreat | Amanti Resort & Spa, Playa Hermosa**

---

## 1. Welcome to Costa Rica!
We are thrilled to welcome you to the sacred biodiversity of Playa Hermosa. Below is everything you need to prepare for an effortless journey.

## 2. Arrival Logistics & Airport Guide
- **Airport:** Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) in San José.
- **Immigration / Customs Form:** Under address in Costa Rica, state: *Amanti Resort & Spa, Playa Hermosa, Puntarenas Province*.
- **Shuttle Pick-up:** Upon exiting SJO customs, walk outside where an Arrive Yoga representative holding an **"Arrive Yoga / Amanti"** sign will meet you.
- **Drive Time:** Approx. 90 minutes direct via paved scenic coastal highway.

---

## 3. Curated Packing Checklist

### Yoga & Movement
- [ ] 5–6 sets of lightweight, breathable yoga / somatic movement wear
- [ ] 2 comfortable swimsuits (for waterfall pools, cold plunge, and beach)
- [ ] 1 light wrap or kimono for morning meditation

### Adventure & Excursions
- [ ] 1 pair of sturdy trail shoes or athletic sneakers (for Rainmaker bridges)
- [ ] 1 pair of water shoes or strap sandals (Tevas/Chacos)
- [ ] Lightweight rain shell or breathable poncho
- [ ] Polarized sunglasses & wide-brim sun hat

### Wellness & Personal Care
- [ ] **Reef-Safe Sunscreen** (Costa Rican marine ecosystems are fragile)
- [ ] Natural insect repellent (lemongrass or eucalyptus based)
- [ ] Personal journal and your favorite pen
- [ ] Reusable insulated water bottle

### Currency & Tech
- [ ] Passport (valid for 6+ months past travel dates)
- [ ] Proof of Travel Medical Insurance
- [ ] Credit cards (widely accepted; notify your bank of Costa Rica travel)
- [ ] $100–$200 USD in cash for personal tips or artisan gifts
