The Art of Arrival • Costa Rica Guest Welcome & Travel Guide • Amanti Resort

"You do not need to arrive healed, fixed, or ready. You only need to arrive willing to breathe."
— Carly Anne Kasinpila, Founder & Lead Facilitator

YOUR DESTINATION: Amanti Resort & Spa, Playa Hermosa, Puntarenas Province, Costa Rica.

AIRPORT: Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) in San José.

TRANSFERS: Coordinated executive private Sprinter & bus shuttles depart SJO for the scenic 90-minute coastal drive.

Travel & Customs Guidelines

  • Passport Validity: Costa Rican immigration requires your passport to be valid for at least 6 months past your travel dates.
  • Customs Address: On your entry form, enter: Amanti Resort & Spa, Playa Hermosa, Puntarenas.
  • Airport Exit: Collect your bags and walk out the main terminal exit. Look for our bilingual greeter holding the Arrive Yoga / Amanti sign.
  • Hydration: Chilled water and fresh tropical elixirs will be provided inside your airport shuttle.

What to Pack

  • 5–6 sets of lightweight, breathable yoga & movement attire
  • 2 swimsuits (for waterfall pools, cold plunge, and black sand beach)
  • Trail running sneakers or strap adventure sandals for Rainmaker bridges
  • Reef-safe sunscreen & natural lemongrass insect repellent
  • Personal journal, pen, and a light wrap or kimono for morning meditation

Resort Sanctuary Amenities

Amanti Resort is reserved exclusively for our private cohort. You will enjoy 2 private waterfall swimming pools, a 12-person hydrotherapy jacuzzi, cold immersion plunge, and an open-air panoramic yoga shala deck nestled under Almendra trees frequented by wild Scarlet Macaws and Toucans.

Cuisine & Dietary Care

Our executive private chef, Chef Mateo Salazar, prepares 3 daily organic farm-to-table meals tailored to your dietary requirements (vegan, gluten-free, keto, vegetarian, or pescatarian). Fresh coconut water and adaptogenic herbal elixirs are served daily.
