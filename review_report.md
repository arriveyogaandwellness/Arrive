## 1. Original Website UX/UI Review & Audit
*Live URL Analyzed:* `https://carly-anne-arrive-yoga.steffano.workers.dev/`

### Strengths of the Existing Foundation
1. **Rich & Thoughtful Copywriting:** Strong trauma-aware philosophy, clear 6-day elemental structure (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Avatar), and detailed host background.
2. **Comprehensive Scope:** Integrates a soul archetype quiz, accommodations, spa treatments, cuisine menu, and Yogis Give Back philanthropy.
3. **Transparent Logistics:** Clear airport pickup guidelines from SJO, daily breakdown, and clear $500 deposit terms.

### Key Friction Points & Redesign Opportunities
1. **Color & Aesthetic Incongruity:** The original site utilized a neon/disco plum aesthetic (`#2d0a3d` plum, `#39ff14` neon green, `#ff007f` neon pink). While energetic, it conflicted with the organic luxury, serene Costa Rican jungle shala, and high-ticket sanctuary experience ($2,200 - $3,100+) expected by discerning wellness travelers.
2. **Visual Hierarchy & Typography:** Monolithic text sections without micro-interactions or breathing room caused visual fatigue.
3. **Generic Stock Media:** Relied on generic stock photos that did not showcase the authentic beauty of Amanti Resort, its two waterfall pools, 2-minute proximity to Playa Hermosa's black sands, or Rainmaker waterfalls.
4. **Lack of Interactive Depth:** Missing dynamic price calculations, multi-sensory audio, and interactive booking customization.

## 2. Amanti Resort & Costa Rica Location Review

- **Location:** Playa Hermosa, Puntarenas Province, Costa Rica. Situated in a lush biodiverse valley just 2 minutes from the secluded volcanic black sand beach and 9 minutes from cascading waterfalls.

- **Capacity & Buyout Exclusivity:** 22 private air-conditioned bedrooms across Honeymoon Suites, Family Suites, 2-bedroom Macaw Villas, 3-bedroom Toucan Villas, and the 5-bedroom Amapola Estate. Ideal for an intimate, co-regulated group container of 20–25 guests.
- **On-Site Amenities:** 
  - 2 waterfall swimming pools & 12+ person hydrotherapy jacuzzi
  - Cold immersion ice bath
  - Open-air panoramic yoga shala deck framed by Almendra trees that attract native wild Scarlet Macaws and Toucans
  - On-site 5-star private restaurant with customizable farm-to-table organic dining (vegan, gluten-free, keto)
- **Nearby Adventures:** Rainmaker Suspended Canopy Bridges (9 min), Tortuga Island Catamaran Sail, Sacred Mayan Temazcal, Sunset Black Sand Horseback Riding.
