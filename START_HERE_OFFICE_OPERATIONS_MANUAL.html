03. SOPs & File Naming Conventions

To ensure effortless document retrieval and team synchronization, all files created by team members must follow standard ISO naming rules:

🎨 Brand Style Governance Portal →

Carly Anne's master intake for styling, color palettes, fonts, and moodboard rules.

Standard Naming Syntax

YYYY-MM-DD_[DEPT]_[Document_Name]_[vX.X].[ext]

Example: 2026-08-15_OPS_CostaRica_Flight_Manifest_v1.csv

Version Control Rules

• Working Drafts: Append _DRAFT to filename.

• Approved Final: Remove draft tag, lock editing, and export PDF.

• Archived: Move legacy versions into department archive folder.
